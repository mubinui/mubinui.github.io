# Design System: Mubin Ul Islam Chowdhury — Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "White Matte Glass on a Lit Field"**

The page is a pale blue-grey room. Four soft light fields (two blue, one indigo, one cyan) are blurred 60px and drift slowly against the scroll. Every piece of content sits in a white matte glass window floating on that field. The glass is frosted rather than glossy: 64% white, a 40px blur with 140% saturation, a fine fractal grain, a 1px bright inner edge, and a wide, soft, low-opacity shadow. The system is visionOS in daylight: windows, capsules, a floating ornament, and grabbers, but light only and quiet. It is a fusion the user directed. The deployed portfolio's composition (portrait with floating facts, bio beside fact tiles, featured project with points, project grid, clustered experience, three research cards, award capsules, a bento photo wall, grouped skills) is rebuilt in this glass.

Density is calm and generous. Centred section heads sit over grids of windows spaced 16 to 24px apart, with 5 to 8rem between sections. Colour is nearly absent. Ink and grey carry the text, a single Apple blue marks action and measured results, and a green appears only for "live" states. Depth comes from blur, the white edge, and a diffuse shadow, plus three motions: windows rising from depth as they enter, the portrait on a slower far plane, and a specular light that follows the pointer across a window's surface and rim.

The confirmed rejections are the white "category standard" build with bands and one blue (not modern Apple enough), the dark Spatial Windows photo environments, and the deployed site's slop: neon orbs, a typed hero, count-up vanity stats, numbered kickers, icon tiles, a fake logo wall, and a CTA banner.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Light only. `color-scheme: light`; theme colour `#eef2f8`.
- One material, white matte glass, used on windows, capsules, pills, tags, the ornament, and the segmented control.
- Continuous rounding: 32px windows and 999px controls.
- Four large grabber-bearing windows (hero, portrait, featured project, contact). All other windows are plain.
- Motion is off under reduced motion. Glass becomes solid white under reduced transparency or when backdrop-filter is unsupported.

## Colors

The page is near-neutral: a cool, pale field, white glass, and graphite ink, with one blue for action and a green for things that are live now.

### Primary
- **Apple Action Blue** (action-blue): the Email pill, the result figures (50%+, 100%, 48 hours), award year badges, the featured project's point bullets, the focus ring, and the skip link. It darkens to **Pressed Blue** (action-blue-hover) on pill hover.
- **Link Blue** (link-blue): inline links, chip-link text, and the outbound arrow glyph. On chips it sits over **Blue Wash** (blue-tint), which deepens on hover (blue-tint-hover).

### Secondary
- **Live Green** (live-green) on **Live Wash** (live-tint), with a **Signal Dot** (live-dot): only the "Now" badge on current roles and the final "live" step of a pipeline flow.

### Neutral
- **Pale Field** (field): the page ground, theme colour and scrollbar track. The ambient layer grades from **Field Top** (field-top) through **Field Mid** (field-mid) to **Field Bottom** (field-bottom).
- **Light Fields** (light-blue, light-indigo, light-cyan): radial gradients that fade to transparent at 65% and are blurred 60px. A second, fainter blue field (0.10 alpha) sits mid-page. They are atmosphere, never objects.
- **Matte Glass** (glass-fill): the fill for every glass surface, layered under an SVG grain at 4.5% alpha. It rises to **Glass Hover** (glass-hover) on hover and falls back to **Solid Glass** (glass-solid) when transparency is reduced.
- **Toolbar Glass** (glass-toolbar): the translucent strip under a work window's spec row.
- **Graphite Ink** (ink): headings and primary text. **Slate Secondary** (ink-secondary): intros, method text, bylines, inactive ornament links.
- **Hairline** (hairline): 1px dividers inside windows (spec rows, point lists, More-work rows, experience clusters). **Tint** (tint): skill tags and flow steps. **Grabber Grey** (grabber): the window grabber bar.

### Named Rules
**The Measured Blue Rule.** Blue marks action, measurement, and focus. It never fills a window, a section band, or a heading.

**The Green Means Now Rule.** Green appears only on something current or live: a present role, or the deployed end of a pipeline.

## Typography

**Display Font:** the platform SF stack (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, SF Pro Display, SF Pro Text) with Helvetica Neue, Segoe UI, Roboto, Arial fallbacks
**Body Font:** the same stack
**Label/Mono Font:** none distinct. Numbers use tabular figures where they align.

**Character:** One family at Apple's own settings: bold, tightly tracked display sizes over 17px body text with -0.022em tracking. No webfont is loaded. The user pinned modern Apple, and whether to add a non-Apple fallback face is still open with them.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, clamp 2.75 to 4.5rem, 1.03, -0.038em): the name in the hero window. The contact window's "Let's talk." uses the same shape one step smaller (display-contact).
- **Result Figure** (700, clamp 2.75 to 4.25rem, 1.0, -0.045em, tabular, action blue): measured results only.
- **Headline** (700, clamp 2.25 to 3.75rem, 1.06, -0.035em): centred section titles, balanced.
- **Title** (700, clamp 1.5 to 2rem, 1.12): project names. The featured project goes larger (title-featured). Roles use 1.25 to 1.5rem.
- **Lede** (600, clamp 1.3125 to 1.75rem, 1.22): the hero thesis line.
- **Window Title** (700, 22px, 1.25): titles inside smaller windows, such as skill groups, research cards, fact tiles, and experience clusters.
- **Intro** (400, 19px, 1.42, secondary ink): the one-line section intro under each headline, max 44em.
- **Claim** (600, 18px, 1.3): a project's one-sentence claim. The same shape at 19px is used for text results.
- **Body** (400, 17px, 1.47): the default. The bio uses **Body Long** (18px, 1.55). Supporting copy uses **Body Small** (15px).
- **Label** (600, 14px): capsules, chips, the ornament (500, 600 when current), and the segmented control.
- **Caption** (13px): spec rows, bylines, fact-tag detail, and wall captions (600).

### Named Rules
**The Earned Figure Rule.** Display-scale blue figures appear only on measured results (50%+, 100%, 48 hours). A result without a number is set as a 19px semibold sentence in ink.

**The One Family Rule.** Hierarchy comes from size, weight, and tracking in SF alone. Don't add a second display face.

## Layout

A single centred column, 1120px wide, with a 20px gutter (safe-area aware). Sections are separated by clamp(5rem, 9vw, 8rem). Each opens with a centred head (headline plus intro, max 44em) and continues as a grid of windows with a clamp(16px, 2vw, 24px) gap.

- **Hero:** a two-column stage (1.4fr : 1fr), at least min(680px, 100svh − 300px) tall. The text window sits left. The portrait window sits right on a far plane, with three glass fact tags floating around it. A centred "Worked with" line follows below.
- **About:** the bio window (1.35fr) beside a 2×2 grid of fact windows.
- **Work:** a full-width featured window (claim, method, result on the left, points on the right), a 2-up grid of work windows, then a More-work list window.
- **Open source:** a full-width lead window over a 2-up grid.
- **Experience:** stacked role windows, with two-column clusters inside.
- **Research:** three cards in a row. **Skills:** three columns, with a full-width education window.
- **Gallery:** centred award capsules, a segmented filter, then a 4-column bento wall of 190px rows with dense flow. Tiles span wide (2 columns), tall (2 rows), or big (2×2) to match the photo's shape.
- **Contact:** one centred window, max 820px.

Fixed chrome: a name capsule top-left and an Email capsule top-right, 14px below the safe area, and the ornament centred 16px above the bottom safe area. The footer reserves 104px so the ornament never covers content.

Responsive: below 860px, every two-column grid stacks and the fact tags drop into a static list under the portrait. The research grid stacks below 960px. Skills go 2-up below 960px and 1-up below 700px. The wall goes to 2 columns (150px rows) below 860px and to 128px rows below 500px. Windows tighten to a 26px radius below 700px. The ornament hides About and Contact below 520px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from material, not stacking. Every glass surface combines a backdrop blur (40px, saturate 140%), a grain-textured 64% white fill, a two-part inner edge (a bright 1px top highlight and a 1px white ring), and one diffuse outer shadow tinted slate (rgba 15, 23, 42), never black. Large windows use a wide, soft drop. Small controls use a tighter drop. The field behind the glass moves: the light fields parallax against the scroll at different rates, and the hero portrait sits on a far plane that lags the scroll by up to ±36px.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Glass Edge** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.95), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.75)`): on every glass surface.
- **Window Drop** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(15,23,42,0.04), 0 24px 60px -24px rgba(15,23,42,0.18)`): windows.
- **Control Drop** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -12px rgba(15,23,42,0.25)`): pills, capsules, round buttons, the ornament, the segmented control, and fact tags. Award capsules use a smaller version (0 6px 18px -10px).
- **Lifted Tile** (`box-shadow: 0 28px 60px -20px rgba(15,23,42,0.45)`): a wall photo on hover, with a scale of 1.02.
- **Selected Thumb** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15,23,42,0.12)`): the ornament indicator and the selected segment.
- **Viewer Photo** (`box-shadow: 0 40px 100px -30px rgba(15,23,42,0.45)`): the enlarged photo in the viewer.

### Named Rules
**The Specular Rule.** With a fine pointer, a non-photo window catches light where the pointer is: a 480px soft white fill and a 240px bright rim, both masked to the window's edge. Photo windows get a static 0.5 white inner ring instead.

**The Materialise Rule.** Windows below the fold enter from depth once, rising from 35% opacity, 28px lower and 97% scale, over 0.9 to 1.1s on cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). None of this happens under reduced motion.

**The Solid Fallback Rule.** Under reduced transparency, or without backdrop-filter support, glass becomes 96% white with no blur.

## Shapes

Everything is continuously rounded, and nothing is square. Windows are 32px (26px below 700px). Controls, chips, tags, badges, the ornament, and caption labels are full capsules (999px). Round buttons are circles. Photos keep a softer, image-specific radius: the portrait 40px (30px on mobile), wall tiles 24px (18px below 500px), and the viewer photo 28px. Floating fact tags are 18px. Borders are internal only: a 1px hairline separates rows inside a window, and the window itself has no stroke apart from its white inner edge. The one recurring silhouette outside the window is the grabber, a 48 × 5px bar with a 3px radius, centred 18px below the four main windows.

## Components

### Buttons
Tactile capsules with Apple's own proportions.
- **Shape:** full capsule (999px), at least 48px tall, padding 12px 26px, 17px semibold.
- **Primary:** action blue with white text. Used only for Email.
- **Glass:** matte glass with ink text, for "See my work" and LinkedIn. It brightens to 90% white on hover.
- **Hover / Focus:** a 0.25s background change. Pressed scales to 0.97. Focus shows a 2px action-blue outline at a 3px offset.
- **Round button:** a 44px glass circle with a 16px stroked SVG glyph. Used for the viewer's close, previous, and next.

### Chips
- **Chip link:** a Blue Wash capsule with Link Blue 14px semibold text and a trailing outbound arrow. Used for Pages, GitHub, and paper links.
- **Skill tag:** a Tint capsule with 14px ink text. Static.
- **Now badge:** a Live Wash capsule with Live Green 12px semibold text and a leading 7px green dot.
- **Flow step:** a Tint capsule at 13px semibold. Steps are joined by a 14px slate connector line, and the last step is green.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 32px windows. Photo windows follow the image radius (see Shapes).
- **Background:** matte glass (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Shadow Strategy:** Glass Edge plus Window Drop.
- **Border:** none. Inside a window, 1px hairline dividers only.
- **Internal Padding:** clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.5rem). The hero window uses clamp(1.75rem, 4vw, 3.25rem).
- **Spec toolbar:** a work window ends in a full-bleed bottom strip (35% white, hairline top, 13px tabular secondary text) that takes the window's lower corners.

### Navigation
- **Top capsules:** two fixed glass capsules at 14px semibold, at least 40px tall. The name shortens to "Mubin" below 600px.
- **Ornament:** a floating glass capsule centred at the bottom, holding six section links (14px, 500, secondary ink). A 95% white thumb with the Selected Thumb shadow slides under the section in view over 0.5s. The current link turns to ink at 600. The thumb hides while the hero is in view.

### Fact Tags
Small glass cards (18px radius, 12px 16px padding) that float around the hero portrait. Each has a 16px bold fact over a 13px secondary source line. Below 860px they become static rows under the portrait.

### Award Capsules
Glass capsules with a leading action-blue year badge (12px bold tabular white) and 14px medium text. They wrap and are centred, max 920px.

### Segmented Control
A glass capsule with a 4px inset. Segments are 36px, 14px medium, secondary ink. The selected segment is solid white with the Selected Thumb shadow, ink text, and 600 weight.

### Bento Photo Wall
Photos in glass photo windows (24px radius, static white inner ring), in a dense 4-column grid whose tile spans follow each photo's aspect. Each tile carries a bottom-left caption capsule (72% white, 20px blur, 13px semibold). Hover lifts the tile. Click opens a full-screen viewer on a blurred pale-field backdrop, with a glass caption capsule, a count, round previous and next buttons, arrow-key navigation, and swipe.

### Window Grabber
A 48 × 5px slate bar centred 18px below a window. It appears only on the hero, portrait, featured project, and contact windows, and is hidden on the portrait when the hero stacks.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put all content in glass windows over the pale field. Every surface uses the one glass recipe: 64% white, grain, blur(40px) saturate(140%), the Glass Edge, and a slate-tinted drop.
- **Do** keep windows at 32px (26px on small screens) and every control a full 999px capsule.
- **Do** reserve action blue for Email, measured result figures, year badges, featured points, and focus.
- **Do** set big blue result figures only on measured results (50%+, 100%, 48 hours). Unmeasured outcomes are a 19px semibold sentence.
- **Do** keep the light fields soft: a radial fade to transparent by 65%, a 60px blur, and at most 0.24 alpha.
- **Do** switch off every motion under prefers-reduced-motion, and solidify glass to 96% white under prefers-reduced-transparency.
- **Do** tint shadows slate (rgba 15, 23, 42), never pure black.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a dark theme or dark sections. The world is light only.
- **Don't** add grabbers to windows beyond the hero, portrait, featured project, and contact windows.
- **Don't** make the light fields saturated, sharp-edged, or neon, and don't turn them into decorative orbs placed on content.
- **Don't** reintroduce the deployed site's slop: a typed hero line, count-up stats, numbered section kickers, icon-per-item tiles, a logo wall, or a CTA banner.
- **Don't** use glossy highlights or gradient fills on glass. The matte grain and white edge carry it.
- **Don't** add a webfont or a second display face without the user's decision. The SF stack is pinned.
