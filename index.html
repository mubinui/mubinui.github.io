Advance Fingerprint Indexing

Large-scale biometric identification and deduplication.

A retrieve-then-rerank cascade: vector and LSH retrieval narrows the gallery, then an exact geometric matcher decides. A ten-finger fusion gate returns an auditable DUPLICATE, CLEAN, or REVIEW verdict, with scale analysis to 10 million records.

100% Rank-1 on FVC2002, and 100% recall@10 at up to 5001× gallery reduction.

  • Java 25
  • SourceAFIS

  • PostgreSQL
  • MinHash-LSH

Scheduled pipelines aggregate live tenders from multiple procurement platforms. A bid-advisory engine matches each company profile against the specification and returns Bid or No-Bid, with email alerts and Admin and Super Admin panels.

48 hours from brief to shipped product. 1st Runner-Up of 33 build-round teams.

Weft

Context, woven: one grounded answer from documents, spreadsheets, and databases.

Hybrid retrieval fuses dense search and BM25 by reciprocal rank. A context graph links every chunk to its section, neighbours, and entities, and personalised PageRank walks it, so one question can hop from a PDF into a database table. Numeric questions become read-only SQL whose result is a citable passage.

It refuses when the evidence is thin. Confidence combines retrieval strength, question coverage, source agreement, and citation rate. It is not the model’s self-report.
