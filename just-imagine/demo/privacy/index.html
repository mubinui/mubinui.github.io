Privacy

Just Imagine stores parent account details, child first names and age bands, saved worlds, discoveries, and session summaries. Child audio is processed for live conversation and is not stored by this application. Child profiles do not need an email address. Parents can remove profiles and family play data in Settings. This local preview stores sample data only in this browser. Production policies and service configuration must be reviewed before release.

Optional photo companions

A parent or guardian can choose a ready-made friend or send a photo to Gemini to create a chibi avatar. The app removes photo metadata and does not save the original photo. The generated artwork is stored in the private child profile and is removed when the profile or account is deleted, or replaced when a different companion is saved. Processing by Gemini is subject to the selected provider service and its data terms.

The companion is an AI play friend. Voice navigation opens child screens; parent settings and payments stay in the parent area. Microphone access requires your browser permission, and you can stop the conversation.
